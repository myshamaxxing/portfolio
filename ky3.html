Brand identity · Mobile web design In progress

Nailed by KY3

A brand identity for my friend Kylie’s nail business: bold, glossy and a little Y2K. A dripping nail-polish logo, a leopard and hot-pink palette, and a gothic display font that matches the attitude of the sets themselves. The first mobile homepage designs put booking and featured work up front.

Logo design Brand identity Mobile UI

Logo

A polish bottle that spells the name

The wordmark pours out of a nail-polish bottle, with the “KY3” letters dripping like fresh polish. I explored it on white, on leopard, and with star accents.

Type & icon

Two fonts, one mark

  • Darkcastle Solid for display: gothic lettering for service names like “Gel X” and “Acrylic.”
  • Tamrin for headings: wide and clean, so it reads at small sizes.
  • The KY3 icon stacks the initials into a compact mark for profile pictures and the site header.

Mobile homepage

Booking first, then the work

The homepage opens on the logo and a Book Now button, then a swipeable gallery of featured sets, then services with starting prices. I’m trying two treatments for the services section: chocolate on a pink gradient, and deep plum.
